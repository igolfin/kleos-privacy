# Privacy Policy – Kleos

Kleos does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Votes in Kleos are anonymous and cannot be linked to individual users or devices. No accounts, emails, names, or identifiers are required to use the app.

Kleos does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

If this policy changes in the future, it will be updated on this page.
